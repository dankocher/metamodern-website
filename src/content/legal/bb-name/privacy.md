This Privacy Policy explains how Metamodern Dev, LLC ("Metamodern Dev," "we," "us," or "our") processes information when
you use the mobile application distributed under names including **bb name** and **Baby Names**, as well as other
localized or platform-specific names depending on the platform, region, and App version (collectively, the "App"). It
also explains the choices and privacy rights that may be available to you.

This Privacy Policy applies to supported Android and iOS versions of the App.

## 1. Who we are

The controller responsible for the processing described in this Privacy Policy is:

**Metamodern Dev, LLC**\
Privacy contact: [metamodern.dev@gmail.com](mailto:metamodern.dev@gmail.com)

## 2. Scope and important distinctions

This Privacy Policy applies to the App, related support communications, the image-delivery infrastructure used by the
App, and the third-party services integrated into the App.

The App does not require an account. Most information you enter or create in the App is stored locally on your device
and is not transmitted to Metamodern Dev. Separately, Google and platform services integrated into the App process
technical, analytics, diagnostic, advertising, consent, and purchase-related information as described below.

## 3. Information stored locally on your device

The App may process and store the following information locally:

- a surname that you choose to enter;
- names you have liked, disliked, or viewed, together with related timestamps;
- name filters and sorting preferences, such as gender, letters, and name length;
- the selected name dataset and application language;
- application state and preferences, including launch and interface counters;
- purchase or subscription entitlement information needed to remember paid access;
- search text while you use the search function. Search text is processed for the requested search and is not
  intentionally sent to our servers or Firebase Analytics.

We do not intentionally send the surname, the names you view, like or dislike, search terms, or filter selections to
Firebase Analytics as custom events or parameters. We do not use this locally stored information to build advertising or
behavioral profiles.

### Device and operating-system backups

Depending on your device, operating-system version, manufacturer, and backup settings, locally stored App data may be
included in a system backup or device-to-device transfer provided by Google, Apple, or another platform provider.
Metamodern Dev does not receive, access, or control these system backups. You can manage available backup settings
through your device or platform account.

You can delete the App's local copy of this information by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by
uninstalling the App. A system backup, if one exists, is controlled separately by the relevant platform provider and
your platform-account settings.

## 4. Name-card sharing

If you choose to share a name card, the App creates a temporary image locally. The image may contain the selected name,
the surname you entered, and other information visible on the card.

The App passes the temporary image to the operating system's share interface only after you initiate sharing. You choose
the receiving application or person. The temporary file is deleted by the App after the sharing operation is completed
or attempted. The recipient application or service processes the shared content under its own privacy policy, and
Metamodern Dev does not control that processing.

## 5. Name images and technical server logs

When you open information about a name, the App may request the corresponding image from `api.metamodern.dev`. If the
primary request is unsuccessful, the App may use a relay hosted on `metamodern.dev`.

As part of an ordinary internet request, our server and infrastructure providers may receive:

- your IP address;
- the date and time of the request;
- the requested image path, which may indicate the name whose image was displayed;
- standard HTTP request information, such as protocol and user-agent information;
- technical error and response information.

These technical logs are used only to deliver images, maintain service availability, diagnose failures, prevent abuse,
and protect infrastructure. They are not combined with the surname or locally stored choices, are not used for
advertising or behavioral profiling, and are not sent to Firebase Analytics by this image-delivery process.

Technical access and error logs are retained for no longer than **30 days**, after which they are deleted or
irreversibly aggregated unless a longer period is necessary to investigate a specific security incident or comply with
law.

## 6. Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics is enabled in the App and automatically processes information about use of the App. Depending on the
device and available settings, this may include:

- an App Instance ID, Firebase Installation ID, advertising identifier, App Set ID, or similar identifiers;
- device model, operating system, language, country or approximate location derived from technical signals, App version,
  and mobile-network information;
- App launches, sessions, engagement, screen or feature interactions, and other automatically collected events;
- advertising impressions and interactions when Analytics is linked with AdMob;
- automatically collected in-app purchase events, as described below.

Firebase Analytics is used to understand aggregate App usage, maintain and improve the App, evaluate feature
performance, and measure advertising and purchase activity. We do not configure custom Analytics events or parameters to
transmit the surname, the names you view, like or dislike, search terms, or filter settings.

The Firebase Analytics property may be linked with AdMob and Google Play. These links allow Google services to exchange
eligible analytics, advertising, attribution, audience, and purchase information in accordance with the configured
Google account settings and Google's terms.

User-level and event-level Analytics data is configured for a **14-month retention period**. The retention period resets
when the user has new activity. This setting does not necessarily control aggregated reports or information retained by
Google for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other purposes described in Google's policies.

Advertising privacy choices presented through AdMob do not necessarily disable Firebase Analytics. Where permitted by
law, we rely on our legitimate interests in understanding App performance and improving reliability. Different legal
bases, including consent, may apply where required by applicable law.

For more information, see [Google's Firebase Privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 7. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics is enabled in release versions of the App and is used to identify, diagnose, and correct crashes
and stability problems. Crashlytics may process:

- crash reports, stack traces, and diagnostic logs;
- App state at the time of a crash;
- device model, operating-system version, App version, and technical configuration;
- Firebase Installation ID and crash-installation identifiers;
- other technical information needed to group and investigate errors.

We do not configure Crashlytics custom keys or logs to include the surname entered by the user, the user's liked or
disliked name selections, search terms, or filter selections.

## 8. Firebase Remote Config and platform delivery services

Firebase Remote Config is used to deliver configuration controlling feature availability and presentation. Remote Config
may process a Firebase Installation ID and technical App or device information needed to deliver configuration values
and prevent abuse. It is not used to upload the surname, name choices, search terms, or filters.

Platform delivery services, such as Google Play services, Play Asset Delivery, the Apple App Store, or related platform
mechanisms, may process limited device metadata, App version information, request status, and diagnostic information to
deliver App components and datasets and to protect the distribution process.
